B.A. (Prog.) with Economics as a Major discipline is one of the largest programmes at Delhi University by enrolment, combining core Economics papers with a second discipline and the university-wide elective pools — a broader, less specialised course load than the Honours programme, but still with real quantitative and theory papers to prepare for.

This guide covers how the programme's Economics papers are structured and where to find genuine previous year question papers.

---

## What the Programme Covers

The programme runs across 8 semesters under DU's UGCF/NEP structure. Economics-as-Major papers cover core micro, macro, and Indian economy theory alongside a lighter quantitative component than the Honours programme, combined with a second major/minor discipline and the university-wide AEC/SEC/VAC/GE papers.

On [DU PYQ Online](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/ba-prog-economics-as-major), the catalogued archive for this programme currently holds **506 previous year question papers across 66 subjects**, organised by semester.

---

## How This Differs From BA Hons Economics

### 1. A Lighter Quantitative Load
B.A. (Prog.) Economics papers generally require less advanced mathematics and statistics than the Honours programme's core, though the underlying microeconomics and macroeconomics theory covered is conceptually similar. Previous year papers are the fastest way to see exactly how much numerical content a specific paper actually expects.

### 2. Papers Still Reward Diagrams and Structure
Even with a lighter syllabus, the same DU-wide pattern holds: theory answers that include correctly labelled diagrams (supply-demand curves, macro models) and clear structure tend to score better than long unstructured prose.

### 3. You're Also Preparing a Second Discipline
Since B.A. (Prog.) combines Economics with another subject, time management across two distinct papers per semester is a real constraint. Reviewing previous year papers for both disciplines early in the semester — rather than economics-first, second-subject-later — avoids a last-minute crunch.

## How to Use Previous Year Papers for This Programme

1. Open your current semester's Economics subjects on the [B.A. Prog. Economics as Major papers page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/ba-prog-economics-as-major).
2. Compare 2–3 years of a paper to gauge how much numerical/graphical content it actually expects versus pure theory.
3. Practice diagram-supported answers for micro/macro theory questions specifically, since these carry dedicated marks in DU's grading pattern.
4. Balance revision time across Economics and your second discipline rather than treating them sequentially.

For general guidance on building a revision plan from previous year papers, see [How to Use DU Previous Year Question Papers for Semester Exams](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/how-to-use-du-previous-year-question-papers).
