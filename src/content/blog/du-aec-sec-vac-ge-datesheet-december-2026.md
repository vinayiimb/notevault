**DU's common papers for December 2026: SEC on 5 Dec (Semester 5), 17 Dec (Semester 1) and 19 Dec (Semester 3); VAC for Semesters 1 and 3 on Monday, 21 Dec; GE between 19 and 26 Dec; and AEC (EVS and language papers) on Saturday, 26 Dec for Semester 1 and Monday, 28 Dec for Semester 3.**

The same dates apply whatever your course (B.A., B.Sc., B.Com, Hons or Prog), because AEC, SEC, VAC and GE papers come from a university-wide pool. The dates below are from the December 2026 datesheet, which DU marks tentative. For your full schedule, core papers included, use the [DU Datesheet page](/exam-help/datesheet) or the [Custom Datesheet Builder](/exam-help/datesheet/custom).

## How to Prepare for the Common Papers

1. **Revise them after your core papers.** Apart from the Semester 5 SEC on 5 December, every common paper comes after mid-December, once your DSC and DSE papers are done.
2. **AEC is your last paper,** on 26 Dec (Semester 1) or 28 Dec (Semester 3). EVS questions repeat a lot from year to year, so two or three [EVS past papers](/papers/ability-enhancement-courses/environmental-science-theory-into-practice-i) are usually enough to see the pattern.
3. **VAC and SEC are 2-credit papers,** but they count toward your SGPA like any other paper. An hour on past papers the day before is the cheapest grade boost you'll get.
4. **Watch for clashes.** In Semester 1, GE (23 Dec) and AEC (26 Dec) are three days apart. In Semester 3, GE (22 Dec) is the day after VAC (21 Dec). Plan your revision around those pairs.

**When is the DU EVS exam in December 2026?**

**When is the DU GE exam in December 2026?**
