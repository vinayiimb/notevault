Semester 3 is where Delhi University gets serious. The introductory-level concepts from first year are now replaced with advanced, specialized content. DSC papers become more analytical, you are choosing your first Discipline Specific Elective (DSE), and the exam questions start expecting deeper understanding rather than textbook definitions.

Many students who coasted through first year with decent grades find Semester 3 to be a rude awakening. This guide shares the strategies you need to maintain — or improve — your SGPA in the second year.

---

## What Changes in Semester 3

### 1. Harder Core Papers (DSCs)
Semester 3 DSC papers build directly on Semester 1 and 2 foundations:
- **Commerce:** Financial Accounting becomes Corporate Accounting. Income Tax Law becomes more computation-heavy.
- **Science:** Organic Chemistry moves to reaction mechanisms. Physics introduces more advanced electrodynamics.
- **Humanities:** Political Theory moves from foundational thinkers to comparative politics. History shifts from ancient to medieval or modern periods.

If your Semester 1–2 fundamentals are weak, Semester 3 will expose those gaps immediately.

### 2. First DSE Selection
Semester 3 is typically when you choose your first **Discipline Specific Elective (DSE)** — an advanced paper within your own discipline. This choice matters because:
- It shapes your specialization for the rest of your degree.
- The DSE carries 4 credits, identical to core DSCs.
- Some DSEs are significantly easier or harder than others.

### 3. The Odd-Semester ER Trap
If you failed any Semester 1 papers, you must re-attempt them during Semester 3 exams. This means you are juggling current Sem 3 papers AND Sem 1 ER papers simultaneously. Plan your study schedule to account for both.

## Semester 3 Study Strategy

### Phase 1: The First 6 Weeks — Close Knowledge Gaps
- Identify any Semester 1–2 concepts that are prerequisites for Semester 3 papers. Spend the first 2 weeks revising these before diving into new content.
- For Commerce students: revise Journal Entries and Ledger Formats before starting Corporate Accounting.
- For Science students: revise basic reaction types before tackling organic mechanisms.

### Phase 2: Weeks 7–12 — Deep Practice
- This is where Semester 3 separates high-scoring students from average ones. Deep practice means:
  - Solving past numerical problems (Commerce/Science) rather than just reading solutions.
  - Writing full essay outlines (Humanities) rather than just highlighting readings.
  - Attempting previous year questions under timed conditions.

### Phase 3: Weeks 13–16 — Exam Execution
- Focus on the recurring question patterns you have identified from [past papers](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes).
- For ER papers: allocate dedicated study slots (e.g., 2 hours daily for the ER paper, separate from your current Sem 3 revision).
- Practice writing complete answers at exam speed — 15 pages in 3 hours requires consistent physical writing practice.

## DSE Selection Tips

When choosing your DSE, consider:

1. **Past paper difficulty.** Before registering, check the [PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes) for the DSE options available in your program. Compare the question difficulty across options.
2. **Faculty reputation.** Ask third-year seniors which DSE faculty are known for fair grading versus harsh marking.
3. **Career alignment.** If you plan to pursue a Master's degree or competitive exams, choose a DSE that aligns with your future goals (e.g., a Commerce student aiming for CA should pick Taxation or Auditing DSEs).

## Handling Semester 1 ERs During Semester 3

If you have Essential Repeat papers from Semester 1:
- Download the specific Semester 1 papers from the [session archive](https://www.dupyq.online/exam-sessions) and review the question patterns.
- Allocate 25% of your total study time to ER revision, and the remaining 75% to current Semester 3 papers.
- The ER exam will use the current Semester 1 syllabus, not the one from your original attempt — confirm the latest syllabus before studying.
