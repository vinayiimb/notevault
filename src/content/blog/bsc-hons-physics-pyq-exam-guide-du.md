B.Sc. (Hons.) Physics at Delhi University builds on a mathematical-methods foundation that underpins every subsequent theory paper — mechanics, electricity and magnetism, thermodynamics, quantum mechanics — which means gaps in the early mathematics papers tend to resurface as difficulty in every physics paper that follows.

This guide covers how the programme's exams are structured and where to find genuine previous year question papers for every subject.

---

## What the Programme Covers

The programme runs across 8 semesters under DU's UGCF/NEP structure, with core DSC theory and practical papers spanning classical mechanics, electromagnetism, thermal physics, and quantum mechanics, supported by mathematical physics papers, plus DSE electives in later semesters and the university-wide AEC/SEC/VAC/GE papers.

On [DU PYQ Online](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bsc-hons-physics), the catalogued archive for this programme currently holds **324 previous year question papers across 52 subjects**, organised by semester.

---

## Why Physics Exams Reward Targeted Problem Practice

### 1. Derivations Recur With Different Given Values
Physics numericals and derivations typically reuse the same underlying setup (a fixed physical scenario or standard derivation) with different given values each year. Working through several years of a paper's numerical section is significantly more efficient than working generic textbook problems.

### 2. Mathematical Methods Papers Set the Ceiling
Since mathematical methods (vector calculus, differential equations, complex analysis) underlie almost every later physics paper, previous year papers for this subject are worth prioritising early — weakness here compounds across the rest of the degree.

### 3. Practicals Are Graded Independently
As with other science programmes at DU, the practical/lab component in semesters that carry one is assessed through viva and lab record separately from the theory paper — theory-paper previous years won't tell you what the practical exam expects.

## How to Use Previous Year Papers for This Programme

1. Open your current semester's subjects on the [B.Sc. Hons Physics papers page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bsc-hons-physics).
2. Work through numerical/derivation questions from at least 3 previous years per paper under timed conditions.
3. Prioritise mathematical methods papers early in the semester, since gaps there affect every later theory paper.
4. Check the paper code and syllabus year on each downloaded PDF against your own — Physics has seen syllabus revisions under UGCF, and older papers may not map exactly to your current paper.

For general guidance on building a revision plan from previous year papers, see [How to Use DU Previous Year Question Papers for Semester Exams](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/how-to-use-du-previous-year-question-papers), and for balancing theory with lab work, see [B.Sc Science Exams: How to Balance Practicals and Theory Prep in DU](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/bsc-science-practical-and-theory-prep-tips-du).
