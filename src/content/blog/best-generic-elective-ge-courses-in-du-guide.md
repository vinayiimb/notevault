Generic Electives (GEs) are one of the most strategically important paper choices you will make at Delhi University. Unlike SECs and VACs (which carry only 2 credits), GEs carry **4 credits** — the same weight as your core Discipline Specific Courses (DSCs). A poorly chosen GE can tank your SGPA, while a well-chosen one can lift it significantly.

This guide breaks down everything you need to know about GEs: what they are, which ones are the easiest to score in, and how to choose the right one for your academic profile.

---

## What is a Generic Elective (GE)?

A Generic Elective is a **4-credit paper offered by a department other than your own**. It is designed to give you interdisciplinary exposure — for example, a History student can take a GE in Economics, or a Commerce student can take a GE in Political Science.

Key facts:
- **Credit Value:** 4 credits (same as core DSC papers — this is why GEs matter so much for CGPA).
- **Assessment:** Full 3-hour written exam + internal assessment. GEs are evaluated exactly like core papers.
- **Number Required:** Most programs require 2 to 4 GEs across the degree.
- **Availability:** GEs are offered by host departments. You must register for them during the official elective selection window.

## The GE Selection Mistake Most Students Make

The most common mistake is choosing a GE purely because it sounds interesting, without checking:

1. **The exam format.** Some GEs have numerical or technical components that are much harder than expected. A History student taking a GE in Economics may enjoy the lectures but struggle with graph-heavy microeconomic theory on the exam.

2. **The grading curve.** When you take a GE from another department, you are graded alongside students from *that* department — students who may have stronger fundamentals in the subject.

3. **Past paper availability.** For GEs, practicing previous year papers is even more critical than for core subjects because you are less familiar with the question patterns. Always check the [DU PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes) before finalizing your GE to confirm that past papers exist and the questions look manageable.

## How GEs Impact Your CGPA

Since GEs carry 4 credits, they have exactly the same CGPA impact as your DSC papers:

- Scoring a **B grade (7 points)** in a GE reduces your SGPA by the same amount as scoring B in a core subject.
- Scoring an **O grade (10 points)** in an easy GE is one of the fastest ways to lift your overall CGPA.

**Strategic tip:** If you are aiming for a 9+ CGPA, your GE should be your easiest paper in the semester. Pick one where you are confident of scoring A+ or O, and use the time saved to invest in your harder DSC papers.
