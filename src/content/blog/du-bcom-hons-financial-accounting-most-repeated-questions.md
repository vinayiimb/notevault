Financial Accounting (Unique Paper Code **2412081103**, DSC-1.3) is the Semester 1 B.Com (Hons) paper where marks depend on practice. Looking at past papers pays off here more than in a theory subject, because the **numericals repeat with only the names and numbers changed.**

We read the three NEP/UGCF papers we have side by side (all say: attempt all questions, with the parts of a question attempted together):

| Paper | Question paper Sr. No. | Listed in our archive as |
|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | 4366 | Nov-Dec 2024 |
| Paper 2 | 851 | 2025 |
| Paper 3 | 7369 | 2026 |

Year labels on uploaded PDFs are not always exact, so go by the Sr. No. printed on the paper. All three are on the [B.Com (Hons) Financial Accounting PYQ page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bcom-hons/financial-accounting).

## Where the same topic came in all three papers

| Topic | Paper 1 (4366) | Paper 2 (851) | Paper 3 (7369) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IFRS** (meaning, need for convergence) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Fair value** | Meaning and advantages | Revaluation journal entries | Short note |
| **Depreciation and the machinery/plant account** (purchases and sales during the year) | Plant destroyed by fire | Machinery sold, method changed | Machinery bought and part-sold |
| **Inventory valuation** | Material cost; petrol inventory | Petrol inventory | Stock as per AS-2; FIFO and weighted average |
| **Revenue recognition (AS 9)** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Final accounts of a sole proprietor from a trial balance** | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Not-for-profit accounts** (income and expenditure, subscriptions, stationery creditors) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Branch accounting** (debtors system) | Delhi to Kolkata | Jaipur and Agra | Delhi to Jaipur |

If you can do these eight types, you have covered most of every paper.

## Questions that were repeated almost word for word

These came in more than one paper, with the wording unchanged or nearly so.

- **"What is meant by IFRS? Why is there a need for convergence?"** Papers 1 and 2 (and Paper 3 asked "need and benefit of IFRS").
- **"What are accounting policies as per AS 5? Explain the considerations in selection of accounting policies. In what conditions should a change be made?"** Papers 1 and 2.
- **"State the meaning of contingent assets. Explain the principles for the measurement of a provision according to AS 29."** Papers 1 and 2.
- **The petrol distributor inventory problem.** "HP is a leading distributor of petrol" (Paper 1) and "Oil India is a leading distributor of petrol" (Paper 2). Same question, different numbers.
- **The club accounts block.** A club with a prize fund and sports fund investments, a club with subscriptions in arrears and in advance, and "creditors for stationery and stock of stationery" with cash purchases at 20 percent of total purchases. Papers 1 and 2 both asked all three, with new names and numbers. Paper 3 asked the subscription, building fund and sports-material-supplier versions.
- **The branch problem.** "X of Delhi invoices goods to its branch at 20% less than the list price, which is cost plus 100%, with cash sales at invoice price and credit sales at list price." Paper 1 (Kolkata) and Paper 3 (Jaipur) use the same set-up.

## True/false in Paper 3: statements and answers

Paper 3 started with nine true/false statements (attempt six, 2 marks each). Seven with clear answers:

| Statement | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Accrual accounting recognises revenue and expenses when earned or incurred, regardless of cash | **True** | That is the definition. |
| GST input tax credit allows a refund of GST paid on purchases | **False** | It is set off against output GST payable, not refunded (refunds apply only in special cases). |
| Expenditure that benefits the current period is revenue expenditure | **True** | |
| The consistency principle allows changing methods without restriction | **False** | Changes need a valid reason and disclosure. |
| Stock is valued at cost or NRV, whichever is higher | **False** | It is whichever is *lower*. |
| A change in the useful life of machinery is a change in accounting estimate | **True** | |
| Outstanding salary is a nominal account | **False** | The *outstanding* amount is a liability (personal account). Salary is the nominal account. |

## The standard answer set to keep ready

**Theory (18 marks in Q1).** Write each of these once, with the standard number: IFRS and convergence, AS 5 (accounting policies, prior period items, changes in estimate), AS 29 (provisions and contingent assets), AS 2 (inventories), AS 9 (revenue), AS 26 (intangibles), fair value, and the accounting principles.

**Numericals.** Practise each type once from a past paper, then redo it with different numbers:

1. Machinery/plant account with purchases and sales and a depreciation rate (with and without a change of method).
2. Inventory valuation: AS-2 (lower of cost and NRV), FIFO and weighted average, and the petrol-distributor type.
3. Final accounts with 5 to 7 adjustments, including GST balances.
4. Club accounts: prize fund, subscriptions (arrears and advance), and stationery and sports-material creditors.
5. Branch accounts under the debtors system, with the memorandum accounts.

## How to use this before the exam

1. **Do the five numerical types first.** They are most of the marks, and they repeat.
2. **Learn the three repeated theory questions** (IFRS, AS 5 policies, AS 29), because two papers asked them in nearly the same words.
3. **Revise the true/false list above.** Short, scoring, and easy to forget under pressure.
4. **Time yourself on one full paper.** Papers 1 and 2 give 3 hours, so keep the last 30 minutes for the branch and final-accounts questions.

Past repetition does not guarantee what comes next. Use this list to decide what to revise first.
