B.Com. (Hons.) at Delhi University combines numerical, ledger-based papers (Financial Accounting, Cost Accounting, Business Mathematics) with theory-heavy management and law papers (Business Laws, Organisational Behaviour, Business Environment) — two very different exam styles running through the same degree, semester after semester.

This guide covers how the programme's papers are structured and where to find genuine previous year question papers for every subject, and links through to a semester-by-semester B.Com preparation strategy already on this site.

---

## What the Programme Covers

The programme runs across 8 semesters under DU's UGCF/NEP structure, with core DSC papers spanning financial and cost accounting, business law, marketing, finance, and management, DSE electives in later semesters, and the university-wide AEC/SEC/VAC/GE papers.

On [DU PYQ Online](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bcom-hons), the catalogued archive for this programme currently holds **361 previous year question papers across 95 subjects**, organised by semester.

## Why Commerce Papers Reward Different Prep for Different Subjects

### 1. Numerical Papers Reuse the Same Problem Formats
Financial Accounting, Cost Accounting, and Business Mathematics questions tend to reuse a fixed set of problem formats (journal entries, ledger preparation, costing methods) with different figures each year. Solving several years of a paper's numerical section is the fastest way to internalise the format.

### 2. Law and Theory Papers Reward Structured, Case-Referenced Answers
Business Laws answers score better when they cite the relevant Act or section alongside the explanation, not just a general description. Previous year papers reveal which specific provisions get asked most often within a broad Act.

### 3. Management Papers Reward Applied Examples
Papers like Organisational Behaviour and Marketing tend to ask you to apply a theoretical model to a scenario, rather than just define it. Previous papers show the pattern of these applied questions, which theory-only revision misses.

## How to Use Previous Year Papers for This Programme

1. Open your current semester's subjects on the [B.Com. Hons papers page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bcom-hons).
2. Solve full numerical problems from 3–4 previous years for accounting and quantitative papers under timed conditions.
3. For law and theory papers, note which specific provisions or models repeat across years and prepare those first.
4. Cross-check the paper code and syllabus year printed on each PDF, since some core accounting papers have had syllabus revisions under UGCF.

For a full semester-by-semester B.Com preparation strategy, see [B.Com Semester Exams Preparation Strategy: Ace DU Commerce Papers](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/bcom-semester-exams-preparation-strategy-du), and for general guidance on using previous year papers, see [How to Use DU Previous Year Question Papers for Semester Exams](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/how-to-use-du-previous-year-question-papers).
