Skill Enhancement Courses (SECs) are among the most misunderstood components of the Delhi University UGCF structure. Many students treat them as throwaway papers, but a poorly chosen SEC can drag your SGPA down by 0.3–0.5 points. On the flip side, a well-chosen SEC is an easy 9 or 10 grade points with minimal effort.

This guide explains exactly what SECs are, lists the most popular options across departments, and shares a strategy for picking the ones that boost your CGPA.

---

## What Exactly is a SEC?

Under the NEP-based Undergraduate Curriculum Framework (UGCF), every student must complete a set number of **Skill Enhancement Courses** across their degree. SECs are designed to build practical, employable skills outside your core discipline.

Key facts about SECs:
- **Credit Value:** 2 credits each (lower than DSC/DSE papers which carry 4 credits).
- **Assessment:** Usually a mix of project work, presentations, and a short theory exam — many SECs have no traditional 3-hour written paper.
- **Offered by:** Multiple departments. You are not restricted to your own department's SECs.

## How to Choose Your SEC: The 3-Question Framework

Before registering, ask yourself these three questions:

### 1. What is the assessment format?
SECs with project-based or presentation-based assessments are generally easier to score in than those with a traditional written exam. Check the syllabus document for your college to confirm the evaluation scheme.

### 2. Who is the faculty?
In many colleges, the same SEC taught by different professors can have vastly different difficulty levels. Ask seniors who took the course last semester about the grading patterns.

### 3. Does the schedule clash with your core papers?
SECs are scheduled alongside your regular timetable. A great SEC is worthless if it conflicts with a DSC tutorial you cannot miss.

## How SECs Affect Your CGPA

Even though SECs carry only 2 credits, they still factor into your SGPA calculation. Since they are typically easier than 4-credit DSC papers, treat them as guaranteed grade-point boosters:

- Scoring an **O (10 grade points)** in a 2-credit SEC is much easier than in a 4-credit DSC.
- Two SECs scoring O grade contribute the same weighted grade points as one 4-credit paper scoring O.
- Neglecting SECs and scoring a B or C grade creates a drag on your overall average that is hard to offset.
