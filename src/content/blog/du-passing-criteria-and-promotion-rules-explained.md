Understanding how Delhi University calculates your marks and determines your eligibility for promotion to the next academic year is crucial for every student. The system operates on specific percentage thresholds, credit requirements, and rules for clearing backlogs. 

This guide breaks down the official DU passing criteria and promotion rules so you can plan your studies and avoid unexpected surprises.

---

## 1. The 40% Passing Rule: Theory vs. Practical vs. Internals

Under the Delhi University Undergraduate Curriculum Framework (UGCF), the passing threshold is **40% overall in each paper**. However, this 40% is subject to specific components:

- **Core Theory Examination:** You must score a minimum of 40% in the semester-end theory examination.
- **Practical Component (if applicable):** For science and applied courses, you must score a minimum of 40% in the practical assessment.
- **Internal Assessment (IA):** Internal assessments (which include class tests, attendance, and assignments) also contribute to your total score. 

### The Split passing condition:
To clear a paper, you must pass **separately in the theory and practical examinations** (if the paper has both). If you fail in either practical or theory, you will receive an **ER (Essential Repeat)** for the entire paper, regardless of how well you did in the other section.

---

## 2. DU Promotion Rules: Moving to the Next Year

Delhi University evaluates promotions on a **yearly basis** rather than a semester basis. You do not get blocked from moving from an odd semester to an even semester (e.g., Semester 1 to Semester 2). 

However, to get promoted from one academic year to the next (e.g., Year 1 to Year 2), you must satisfy the credit requirements:

### Credit and Paper Requirements for Promotion:
To be promoted to the next year, you must pass at least **50% of the total papers** across both semesters of the current academic year. 

For instance, in Year 1:
- If you have a total of 14 papers (7 in Semester 1 and 7 in Semester 2), you must pass a minimum of **7 papers** combined to get promoted to Year 2.
- If you pass fewer than 50% of the papers, you will be flagged as **Failed** and must repeat the year (called a "Year Back"), or register as an ex-student to clear the backlogs.

---

## 3. What is an Essential Repeat (ER) and how to resolve it?

If you do not secure the 40% passing marks in a paper, or if you fail to attend the exam, it is designated as an **Essential Repeat (ER)**. 

### Rules for clearing an ER:
- **Odd Semester ERs** (Semesters 1, 3, 5) can only be re-attempted in the subsequent **odd semester examinations** (e.g., a Sem 1 ER is retaken during Sem 3 exams).
- **Even Semester ERs** (Semesters 2, 4, 6) can only be re-attempted in the subsequent **even semester examinations** (e.g., a Sem 2 ER is retaken during Sem 4 exams).
- You are allowed a maximum span period (typically 6 years for a 3/4-year degree) to clear all your ERs and finish your graduation requirements.

---

## 4. Improvement Examinations

If you have passed a paper but scored poorly, DU allows you to apply for an **Improvement Exam** to boost your CGPA.
- You can take improvement exams for odd semester papers during the next odd semester exams, and even semester papers during the next even semester exams.
- The higher of the two scores (original vs. improvement attempt) will be considered in your final mark sheet.
- You cannot apply for improvement in practical components or internal assessments; improvements only apply to the theory exams.
