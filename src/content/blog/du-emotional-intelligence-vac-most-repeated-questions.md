Emotional Intelligence (UPC **6967000005**) is a Value Addition Course that students from any stream can take in semesters 1 to 4. It has no calculations, so the exam is about knowing the models and writing structured answers with examples.

We collected every paper we have for this code, from the Nov-Dec 2022 sitting to May-June 2026. That is **29 question papers**. Many of them come in pairs: the 70-mark paper reuses the 30-mark paper as its Part A. Counting each pair once leaves **23 different question sets**, and the counts below use those 23.

All of them are free on the [Emotional Intelligence VAC PYQ page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/value-addition-courses/emotional-intelligence). Open a few alongside this post.

A note on the counts: "13 of 23" means 13 of the 23 question sets mention that topic somewhere (a short note, a 10-mark question or part of one), not 13 exam sittings. Years are the labels on our paper pages: some college-library scans are labelled with the year they were scanned, so a paper marked 2024 may be from the sitting before.

## What the paper looks like

There are two versions:

| Version | Time | Marks | Structure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short paper | 1 hour | 30 | **Q1 compulsory: short notes on any two of three (5 + 5)**, then any two of Q2 to Q4 at 10 marks each |
| Long paper | 2.5 hours | 70 | **Part A (30 marks)**, same pattern as the short paper, plus **Part B (40 marks)**: any four questions at 10 marks each |

Short notes ("Write short notes on any two of the following") appear in **every one of the 23 sets**. That is your guaranteed 10 marks, so prepare all the commonly asked notes (below) in 5 to 6 lines each.

These are the same question (sometimes word for word), found in papers from different years.

- **"How do teamwork and collaboration facilitate our social competence? Discuss."** is in May-June 2024, May-June 2025, Nov-Dec 2025 and May-June 2026. Four sittings, same question.
- **The mixed model and social competence.** "How does the 'Mixed model of Emotional Intelligence' contribute to our understanding of social competence?" is in Nov-Dec 2025 and May-June 2026. "What is the Mixed Model of EI, and how does it differ from the Ability Model?" is in May-June 2024 and May-June 2025.
- **"How knowing one's strengths and areas of development can enhance personal competence"** goes back to Nov-Dec 2022 and comes back in 2024, 2025 and 2026.
- **"Define emotional intelligence and discuss the various conflict management styles in detail"** is in May-June 2025 and May-June 2026.
- **"Discuss the role of EI in managing professional relationships. Explain using any one model of EI"** is in a 2025 paper and a May-June 2026 paper, along with **"Can emotional intelligence help in managing stressful life situations?"** and **"Explain any two techniques of relationship management."** Three questions from the 2025 set were reused in the 2026 papers.
- **"Explain how empathy and compassion play a role in the development of social awareness."** is in Nov-Dec 2023 and Nov-Dec 2024 (it appears in two of the Nov-Dec 2024 sets).
- **"The management of our emotions is critical to success. Justify this statement"** is in Nov-Dec 2023 and Nov-Dec 2024.
- **"Socially competent persons communicate effectively and are skilled at conflict management. Elaborate"** is in Nov-Dec 2023 and Nov-Dec 2024.

The pattern: the questions are rotated within a small pool. Practise the ones above and you have most of the question space covered.

## The models in one table (for 10-mark answers)

Model questions come up often ("Describe any two models", "Compare the Ability and Trait models", "Explain using any one model"), so learn this table.

| Model | Who | Core idea |
|---|---|---|
| **Ability model** | Mayer and Salovey | EI is a set of four abilities: **perceiving**, **using**, **understanding** and **managing** emotions |
| **Mixed model** | Daniel Goleman | Four domains: **self-awareness, self-management** (personal competence) and **social awareness, relationship management** (social competence) |
| **Mixed model** | Bar-On | "Emotional-social intelligence": intrapersonal, interpersonal, stress management, adaptability and general mood |
| **Trait model** | Petrides | EI as personality traits, measured by self-report |

Use the table to answer "compare ability and trait models" and "how does the mixed model differ from the ability model" in one sentence each, then add an example.

## How to use this for your exam

1. **Write one clean answer for each question in the repeated list above.** That is about 12 answers, and they cover most 10-mark questions.
2. **Prepare short notes on anger, communication, stress, empathy and Bar-On.** Any two are asked in nearly every paper.
3. **Always add an example.** Many questions say "give examples" or "with suitable examples", and answers without one lose marks.
4. **In the 70-mark paper, don't skip Part B.** Several Part B questions (self-awareness, teamwork, the mixed model) repeat from earlier years.

Past repetition does not guarantee what comes next. Use this list to decide what to revise *first*.
