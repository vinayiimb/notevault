Scoring a 9.0+ SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) in Delhi University is a challenging milestone, but it is entirely achievable. While passing an exam requires basic study, securing an 'Outstanding' (O) or 'Excellent' (A+) grade requires a strategic, analytical approach. 

Top-performing students do not necessarily study longer hours; rather, they understand how to maximize points across every component of the grading system.

This guide shares the blueprint for scoring a 9.0+ SGPA in your semester exams.

---

## 1. The Math of a 9+ SGPA: Target Setting

In the UGCF grading scheme, your final SGPA is a weighted average of your grade points across all papers. To score a 9+ SGPA:
* You need to target **A+ (9 grade points)** or **O (10 grade points)** in your core papers (DSCs).
* You must avoid dropping below **A (8 grade points)** in any paper, including electives (GEs) and skill papers (SECs/VACs). A single poor grade in a 2-credit course can lower your overall average.

---

## 2. Maximize Your Internal Assessment Marks

Many students focus solely on the 75-mark semester-end theory exam. However, **internal assessments contribute up to 25% to 40% of your total marks** (depending on the course structure). 

Securing a high score in internals provides a buffer for the theory exams.

How to secure top marks in internals:
* **Attendance:** DU awards up to 5 marks simply for attendance. Make sure you meet the 85%+ threshold to secure full marks.
* **Assignments and Presentations:** Treat assignments seriously. Cite academic sources, present them in a clean format, and use diagrams or slides for presentations.
* **Class Tests:** Do not treat class tests as informal assessments. Revise core topics beforehand, as these marks are submitted directly to the university portal.

---

## 3. The Exam Booklet Strategy (Theory Paper Execution)

How you write your paper determines how it is graded. Follow these guidelines during the 3-hour exam:

### I. The Order of Attempt
* Start with your strongest answer. First impressions matter—a well-structured, clean first answer builds a positive impression with the examiner.
* Answer questions in descending order of marks (e.g., attempt 15-mark questions first, then 10-mark, then 5-mark). This ensures that if you run out of time, you lose lower-value questions rather than high-value ones.

### II. Time Boxing
* Keep a watch on your desk and allocate time strictly:
  - 15-mark question: 35–40 minutes
  - 10-mark question: 25 minutes
  - 5-mark question: 12 minutes
* Stick to these limits. If you run out of time on a question, leave a blank page and move on to the next. You can return to complete it if you have time at the end.

### III. Formatting and Visual Presentation
* Never write long, unstructured blocks of text. Divide your answers into sections with clear subheadings.
* Draw flowcharts, tables, or mind maps to explain concepts. A visual diagram breaks up the text and helps highlight key points quickly.

## 4. The PYQ Feedback Loop

To score a 9+ SGPA, do not rely on passive reading. Use previous year question papers to build active recall:
1. Download the last 5 years of past papers from the [DU PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes).
2. Set a timer for 3 hours and attempt a complete past paper in a quiet room.
3. Grade your attempt objectively. Check for completeness, answer length, and accuracy of formulas or citations.
4. Focus your revision on the areas where you made mistakes or struggled with timing.
