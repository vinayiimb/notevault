## Where the DU Datesheet Actually Comes From

Every session, DU's Examination Branch publishes the datesheet as a set of programme-wise PDFs on the official portal, **[misc.exam.du.ac.in](https://misc.exam.du.ac.in/)**, organised under three curriculum frameworks that run in parallel across different batches:

- **NEP-UGCF-2022** — the current framework for most undergraduate batches (B.A. Hons, B.Sc. Hons, B.Com Hons, B.A./B.Sc./B.Com Programme, Vocational Studies, and all the cross-cutting GE/DSE/AEC/SEC/VAC papers)
- **CBCS-LOCF** — older-batch students still finishing out their programme under the previous curriculum
- **CBCS-LOCF-SOL** — School of Open Learning students, published separately from Regular-mode papers

That's **19 separate programme categories**, each its own PDF, each running into hundreds of individual paper-code entries. Finding your one exam date inside a 500-row PDF, sorted by paper code rather than by your subject name, is the actual problem most students run into — which is exactly what our datesheet tool exists to fix: we've already pulled every entry from all 19 official PDFs into one searchable, per-programme table.

## Important: The Datesheet Is Tentative

DU's own portal explicitly flags this datesheet as tentative and advises students to **check regularly for revisions**. In practice, this means:

- Individual paper dates can shift by a few days if there's a clash or a college-specific issue
- A **revised datesheet** is sometimes issued closer to the exam window — always for specific programmes/papers, not usually a full re-release
- The safest habit is to re-check your date sheet about a week before your first paper, and again a day or two before each individual exam

If you bookmark our datesheet page, you can just re-search your programme each time rather than re-downloading PDFs from the university portal.

## Regular vs. SOL vs. NCWEB: Know Which One Applies to You

A lot of the confusion around "the DU datesheet is wrong" or "I can't find my paper" comes from checking the wrong mode:

| Student type | Datesheet framework | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Regular college student (on-campus) | NEP-UGCF-2022 or CBCS-LOCF, depending on your batch year | Programme-wise PDF under Regular mode |
| School of Open Learning (SOL) | CBCS-LOCF-SOL | Separate SOL-specific PDF, even for the same programme name |
| NCWEB | Listed within the relevant programme category, marked NCWEB | Same portal, NCWEB-tagged entries |

If you're a SOL student searching by your programme name and not finding your paper, you're very likely looking at the Regular-mode file — search specifically for the SOL variant instead. Our datesheet browser separates these clearly by mode so you don't have to guess.

## What to Do Once You Have Your Date

Finding the date is step one. Here's what matters next:

1. **Cross-check against your admit card** once it's released — the admit card is the final word on your exact date, time, and seat, and should match the datesheet, but confirm before exam week. See our [Admit Card guide](https://www.dupyq.online/exam-help/admit-card) if you haven't downloaded yours yet.
2. **Work backwards from your first exam date** to build a study schedule — our [Odd Semester Exam Preparation Roadmap](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/du-odd-semester-exams-2026-preparation-roadmap) has a week-by-week framework you can slot your real dates into.
3. **Start solving previous year papers for your exact subjects** — the [DU PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes) has previous years' papers organised the same way as the datesheet, by programme and semester, so once you know your papers you can go straight to practicing them.
4. **If a paper date clashes or looks wrong**, don't assume — check the official portal directly and, if needed, raise it with your college's examination coordinator early, not the week of the exam.

## Quick Answers

**Is the DU datesheet released for all semesters at once?**
Typically, odd semesters (1, 3, 5, 7) are the main December session; even-semester and backlog/ER papers for the same window are usually listed within the same release cycle but check your specific programme's PDF, since timing can vary slightly by category.

**Why can't I find my paper code on the university PDF?**
Paper codes in the official PDFs are often unique-paper-code (UPC) style, not your subject's common name — search by your DSC/DSE/GE code from your college enrolment, or use our tool, which is searchable by subject name.

**Does the datesheet show exam timing/shift?**
Yes — each entry includes the date and shift/timing alongside the paper. Both are pulled through on our datesheet page.
