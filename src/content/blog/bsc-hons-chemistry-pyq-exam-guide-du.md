B.Sc. (Hons.) Chemistry at Delhi University splits its workload across three distinct sub-disciplines every semester — Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry — each with its own theory paper and, in most semesters, an attached practical component. Balancing all three at once is the core challenge of the degree.

This guide covers how the programme's exams are structured and where to find genuine previous year question papers for every subject.

---

## What the Programme Covers

The programme runs across 8 semesters under DU's UGCF/NEP structure, with core DSC theory and practical papers in Physical, Organic and Inorganic Chemistry, DSE electives in later semesters, and the university-wide AEC/SEC/VAC/GE papers.

On [DU PYQ Online](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bsc-hons-chemistry), the catalogued archive for this programme currently holds **382 previous year question papers across 65 subjects**, organised by semester.

## Why Chemistry Exams Reward a Specific Study Pattern

### 1. Mechanisms and Numericals Repeat by Type
Organic Chemistry questions on reaction mechanisms and Physical Chemistry numericals tend to reuse the same underlying reaction types or formulas across years, with different specific molecules or values. Working through several years of a paper's numericals is far more useful than working through a textbook's generic problem sets.

### 2. Inorganic Chemistry Rewards Structured Recall
Inorganic Chemistry answers on bonding, coordination compounds, and periodic trends are usually assessed on structured, point-wise recall rather than long-form argument — previous papers show the expected answer length and level of detail examiners are looking for.

### 3. Practical Exams Are Assessed Separately
Where a semester has an attached practical paper, its viva and record-based assessment is evaluated independently of the theory paper. Previous year theory papers won't tell you what's asked in the practical, so treat lab manual preparation as a separate task.

## How to Use Previous Year Papers for This Programme

1. Open your current semester's subjects on the [B.Sc. Hons Chemistry papers page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/bsc-hons-chemistry).
2. Work through numerical questions from at least 3 previous years per paper under timed conditions before your exam.
3. For Organic Chemistry, track which reaction mechanisms recur most often across years and prioritise those.
4. Confirm your paper code against the syllabus year printed on each downloaded PDF — Chemistry papers have been revised more than once under UGCF, and older PDFs may not match your current syllabus exactly.

For general guidance on building a revision plan from previous year papers, see [How to Use DU Previous Year Question Papers for Semester Exams](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/how-to-use-du-previous-year-question-papers), and for balancing theory with lab work specifically, see [B.Sc Science Exams: How to Balance Practicals and Theory Prep in DU](https://www.dupyq.online/blog/bsc-science-practical-and-theory-prep-tips-du).
