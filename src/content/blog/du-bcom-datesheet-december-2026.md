**DU B.Com exams for December 2026 start on Friday, 4 December (Semester 5, Income Tax Law and Practice) and the core papers end on Friday, 18 December.** B.Com (Hons) papers are at **9:30 AM** and B.Com (Prog) papers at **2:30 PM** (Semester 7 B.Com (Prog) is at 9:30 AM). Your SEC, VAC, GE and AEC papers come after that, ending with AEC on 26 December (Semester 1) or 28 December (Semester 3).

Every date below is from the university's December 2026 datesheet (tentative, as DU always marks it). Each paper links to its **previous year question papers**, so you can open the PYQs for the exam you're preparing for next. To get only your own papers in order, use the [Custom Datesheet Builder](/exam-help/datesheet/custom).

| Semester 5 | Sat, 5 Dec (2:30 PM) | — | Thu, 24 Dec | — |
| Semester 7 | — | — | Sat, 19 Dec & Sat, 26 Dec | — |

GE and AEC papers run in both the 9:30 AM and 2:30 PM slots depending on the paper, so check your exact paper. Paper-wise lists are in the [DU AEC, SEC, VAC & GE datesheet guide](/blog/du-aec-sec-vac-ge-datesheet-december-2026).

So a **Semester 1 B.Com (Hons)** student's December looks like this: Management Principles (7 Dec) → Financial Accounting (14 Dec) → SEC (17 Dec) → VAC (21 Dec) → GE (23 Dec) → AEC (26 Dec).

## How to Use the Gaps Between Papers

- **Semester 5 is front-loaded.** Income Tax (4 Dec), the SEC (5 Dec), Business Economics (9 Dec) and Management Accounting (12 Dec) come in the first nine days. Start Income Tax now: the [Income Tax Law and Practice PYQs](/papers/bcom-hons/income-tax-law-and-practice) show which computation questions come back every year.
- **Semester 1 has a full week before Financial Accounting.** You have 7 days between Management Principles and Financial Accounting. Spend them solving [Financial Accounting past papers](/papers/bcom-hons/financial-accounting) under time limits. Reading notes won't prepare you for the numericals.
- **Semester 3 has three DSCs in eight days** (8, 10 and 15 Dec). Work through [Business Mathematics PYQs](/papers/bcom-hons/business-mathematics) first, because it's the first paper and the one where practice matters most.
- **Common papers come last.** SEC, VAC, GE and AEC fall in the second half of December, so revise them after your core papers, not before.

For a broader revision plan, see [B.Com semester exam preparation strategy](/blog/bcom-semester-exams-preparation-strategy-du) and [how to use DU previous year papers](/blog/how-to-use-du-previous-year-question-papers).

## Quick Answers

**When do DU B.Com exams start in December 2026?**
Friday, 4 December 2026, with Semester 5 Income Tax Law and Practice for both B.Com (Hons) and B.Com (Prog).

**What time are the DU B.Com exams?**
B.Com (Hons) papers are at 9:30 AM and B.Com (Prog) papers at 2:30 PM. B.Com (Prog) Semester 7 is at 9:30 AM.

**When is the last B.Com exam?**
Core and DSE papers end on Friday, 18 December (Semester 3 DSE). Common papers continue after that: AEC is on 26 December for Semester 1 and 28 December for Semester 3.

**Is the DU datesheet final?**
DU releases it as tentative and occasionally reschedules papers. Check the [DU Datesheet page](/exam-help/datesheet) before each exam, and confirm on exam.du.ac.in.

**My paper isn't listed. What now?**
Some papers appear only on a separate programme's datesheet or in a later revision. Search for your paper code on the [DU Datesheet page](/exam-help/datesheet). How the datesheet works: [how to check the DU datesheet](/blog/du-datesheet-december-2026-how-to-check).
