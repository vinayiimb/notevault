Value Addition Courses (VACs) are among the lightest papers in the Delhi University UGCF structure — yet they can make or break the difference between an 8.5 and a 9.0 SGPA. Most students barely think about VACs until registration week, then scramble to pick whatever slot is available. That is a mistake.

This guide explains exactly what VACs are, lists the most popular options, and shares strategies to guarantee a high grade with minimum effort.

---

## What is a VAC?

A Value Addition Course (VAC) is a **2-credit, non-discipline-specific course** designed to promote holistic development, constitutional awareness, ethical reasoning, and physical fitness. Think of it as DU's way of ensuring students develop soft skills alongside their academic specialization.

Key characteristics:
- **Credit Value:** 2 credits (same as SEC).
- **Assessment:** Usually internal assessment-heavy — attendance, participation, short assignments, and a brief end-term exam or presentation. Many VACs do not have a formal 3-hour written paper.
- **Grading Impact:** Since VACs are typically easy to score in, they act as CGPA boosters when chosen wisely.

## How VACs are Graded

Most VACs follow a simple grading breakdown:
- **Attendance:** 20–30% of total marks. Simply showing up consistently guarantees a significant chunk of your grade.
- **Internal Assignments:** 30–40% of total marks. These are usually short reflective essays, group presentations, or practical demonstrations.
- **End-Term Assessment:** 30–40% of total marks. Often a short written test (1 hour) or a project submission.

### The Key Insight
Since attendance and assignments together account for 50–70% of the total marks, a student who attends regularly and submits assignments on time is almost guaranteed a minimum A grade (8 grade points) even before the end-term assessment.

## VAC Selection Strategy

### Step 1: Check Your College's Offerings
Not every college offers every VAC. Check your college notice board or departmental website during the registration window.

### Step 2: Ask Seniors About Grading Leniency
The same VAC can have drastically different grading standards depending on the faculty member. A professor who gives full attendance marks for 75% attendance is very different from one who requires 90%.

### Step 3: Avoid Time Conflicts
VACs are scheduled alongside your regular classes. A VAC that conflicts with a DSC lecture or tutorial is not worth the CGPA boost if it causes attendance issues in your core paper.
