This guide covers the expected timeline, strategic preparation approaches, and everything you need to know about the 2026 odd semester exams.

## Semester-Wise Focus Areas

### Semester 1 (First-Year Students)
- **Challenge:** Adapting to university-level exams for the first time.
- **Focus:** Master the basics of your DSC papers. Semester 1 lays the foundation for everything that follows.
- **Action:** Download Semester 1 past papers now from the [DU PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes) and familiarize yourself with the question format.

### Semester 3 (Second-Year Students)
- **Challenge:** Significantly harder DSC papers + first DSE selection.
- **Focus:** Bridge knowledge gaps from Semester 1–2 before diving into advanced Semester 3 content.
- **Action:** If you have Semester 1 ERs, start revising for those now — you will re-attempt them during Sem 3 exams.

### Semester 5 (Third-Year Students)
- **Challenge:** Most advanced papers + career pressure (placements, entrance exams).
- **Focus:** Strict time management between academics and career preparation.
- **Action:** Identify the highest-weight papers in your Semester 5 and allocate proportional study time.

## The 8-Week Preparation Framework

If you start serious preparation 8 weeks before exams (typically early October), here is how to structure your time:

### Weeks 1–2: Audit Phase
- Complete any pending internal assessment submissions (assignments, projects).
- Gather all study materials: notes, readings, reference books.
- Download past 3–5 years of papers from the [PYQ archive](https://www.dupyq.online/pyq-notes).
- Build a topic-frequency table for each paper to identify high-yield topics.

### Weeks 3–4: Foundation Phase
- Read through all units systematically. Focus on understanding concepts, not memorization.
- For numerical papers: solve 5 practice problems per topic per day.
- For theory papers: create one-page summary notes for each unit.

### Weeks 5–6: Active Recall Phase
- Stop reading and start practicing. Attempt past paper questions from memory.
- Write full-length answers for 15-mark questions to practice writing speed.
- Use the [exam kit tools](https://www.dupyq.online/tools/exam-kit) to drill recurring definitions and formulas.

### Weeks 7–8: Simulation Phase
- Solve 2 complete previous year papers per subject under strict 3-hour exam conditions.
- Review your mock attempts and identify weak areas. Focus final revision on those areas.
- The night before each exam: review your summary sheets only. Do not read new material.
