Political Leadership and Communication (UPC **3206000002**) is a Skill Enhancement Course open to students of many programmes. It is a pure essay paper with no calculations or practical writing, so the best preparation is knowing which essays the paper keeps returning to.

We collected every paper we have for this code, from papers labelled 2023 to May-June 2026, and removed duplicate scans. That left **25 distinct question papers**. All of them are free on the [Political Leadership and Communication SEC PYQ page](https://www.dupyq.online/papers/skill-enhancement-courses/political-leadership-and-communication). Open a few alongside this post.

Two notes on the counts. "18 of 25" means 18 of the 25 distinct papers mention that topic somewhere, not 18 exam sittings. And years are the labels on our paper pages: some college-library scans are labelled with the year they were scanned, so a paper marked 2025 may be from the sitting before.

---

## What the paper looks like

Every recent paper is the same:

- **1 hour, 30 marks.**
- **Five or six essay questions. Attempt any three. Each carries 10 marks.**
- All questions are descriptive ("Discuss", "Examine", "Analyse", "Critically evaluate"). One paper (Nov-Dec 2025) also has a short-notes question (any of: Psephology, Charismatic Leadership).
- The two oldest papers we have were 2 hours, 50 marks, attempt any three (they use the older paper code 3206001002).

You write three essays, so you can leave half the paper. The question is which half.

## DU's syllabus maps onto the questions

DU's syllabus for this course has five units, and nearly every question belongs to one:

| Unit | What it covers | Typical question |
|---|---|---|
| I | Meaning, nature, scope and evolution of Political Communication | "Define Political Communication and discuss its changing nature" |
| II | Leadership: themes, theories, typologies, participation and performance | "Discuss theories of Political Leadership with examples" |
| III | Developing and strengthening communication and leadership through research | "Techniques that strengthen communication and leadership" |
| IV | Survey research, opinion polls, exit polls, contemporary challenges | "Opinion polls and exit polls ... critically evaluate" |
| V | Psephology as an art and a vocation; career options | "Psephology is both a vocation and a profession. Examine" |

If you only have a week: **psephology (what it is, voting behaviour, democratic participation), nature and scope of Political Communication, leadership theories and typologies, communication and leadership together, and public policy.** Those five cover nearly every paper.

## What changed in 2026

The three May-June 2026 papers are the newest we have, and they look different:

- **None of them asks about psephology, opinion polls, exit polls or survey research.** Those topics are in 18 of the 25 papers overall.
- **They lean on newer angles:** "narrative-building and agenda-setting", "the influence of technology on Political Communication", "the issue of misinformation", "the impact of globalisation on political leadership styles", "the role of media institutions in shaping political narratives" and "new leadership patterns in the 21st century".

This does not mean psephology is gone (the Nov-Dec 2025 paper had it as a short note), but it is a reason to prepare **both** the classic topics and the newer ones.

## Questions that were repeated across papers

These are the same question (sometimes word for word), found in papers from different years.

- **"What is Political Communication? Discuss various models of Political Communication"** is word for word in May-June 2025 and in a 2025 paper.
- **"Define Political Communication. Explain / Discuss its changing nature (in contemporary times)"** is in two 2025 papers, with "Define PC. Explain its nature and significance in contemporary times" and "Discuss the nature and scope of PC" (word for word in an older 2-hour paper and in Nov-Dec 2024) as close cousins.
- **"Discuss different modes of (effective) Political Communication"** is in two 2025 papers and in Nov-Dec 2024.
- **"Define Leadership? Discuss various forms of Political Leadership in liberal democracies"** (2025) and **"What is meant by leadership? Explain the different types of political leadership found in liberal democratic systems"** (May-June 2025) are the same question.
- **Communication and leadership:** "Communication is an important skill of leadership / required to achieve leadership" is in 2024 and 2025; "Effective communication is essential for successful leadership" is in May-June 2025; "Effective Political Communication is essential for strong leadership" is in 2024.
- **Leadership quality and political participation:** "The quality of Political Leadership in democratic countries is an important factor influencing political participation" is in 2025; "Examine how the quality of political leadership in democratic nations influences political participation" is in May-June 2025; "Performance of Political Leadership is an important factor influencing political participation" is in 2024.
- **"Psephology as a tool to understand voting behaviour"** is in May-June 2024 ("What is voting behaviour? How can psephology be used as a tool to study voting?"), Nov-Dec 2024 ("What is Psephology? How can it be used to assess voting behaviour?"), 2024 and 2025.
- **"Psephology can be used as a tool for deepening democratic participation"** (2024) and **"Democratic participation can be strengthened with Political Communication and psephology"** (2025) are the same idea.
- **Public policy as a tool of Political Communication** is in 2024 ("Public Policy can be an effective tool of Political Communication") and 2025 ("How public policies can be used as effective tools of Political Communication?").
- **"Define survey research ..."** is a repeating opener: Nov-Dec 2024, and again in two 2025 papers (each adds a different second half: challenges, utility in voting behaviour, changes in India).
- **Globalisation and Political Communication:** "Discuss the effects of globalisation with reference to Political Communication" (May-June 2024), "Political Communication has reoriented with the advent of technology in the era of Globalisation" (2024), "Globalisation has opened new channels of Political Communication" (an older 2-hour paper).

The pattern: the same ten or so questions are rewritten with new openings. If you can answer the ones above, you can answer most of the paper.

## How to use this for your exam

1. **Write one clean essay for each of the five revision topics** (psephology, nature and scope of PC, leadership theories, communication and leadership, public policy). You need only three essays on the day.
2. **Prepare the 2026 topics too:** media and narrative-building, misinformation, technology and globalisation, new leadership patterns. They are what the newest papers asked.
3. **Learn a few real examples:** an election, a leader, a speech, the 2024 Lok Sabha campaign. The questions often say "with examples" or "with reference to".
4. **Open each answer with a one-line definition,** then the theory or model, then the example. Short, structured essays score better than long ones.

Past repetition does not guarantee what comes next. Use this list to decide what to revise *first*.
