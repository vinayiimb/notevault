Question No. 1
Painter A can paint a building in 12 days while Painter B can paint it in 16 days. If A and B work on alternate days, and A starts the work on the first day, then the number of days required to paint the building is
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Total work = LCM(12,16) = 48 units. A efficiency = 4, B efficiency = 3. Work in 2 days = 7 units. In 12 days = 42 units. Day 13 A does 4 units (46 total). Day 14 B does remaining 2 units taking 2/3 day. Total time = 13 3/4 days.
