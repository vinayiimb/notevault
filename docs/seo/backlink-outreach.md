# Backlink outreach templates

Ethical, manual outreach only. Every message here is meant to be sent one at a
time, by a person, to a real contact who runs or moderates the destination
page — never as bulk/automated email blasts.

**Do not use these templates to:**

- request a dofollow link, a reciprocal link, or a link exchange
- offer payment, free "premium" access, or any other incentive for a link
- imply urgency, scarcity, or a deadline that doesn't exist
- claim endorsement, partnership, or affiliation with the recipient's
  college/organisation unless that's true
- send the same message to dozens of recipients in one sitting — personalise
  the opening line with something specific to their page/society first

If a recipient doesn't respond, follow up once, politely, after a couple of
weeks — then let it go. No link is owed to us; every "yes" is intentionally a
favour to their students, not a completed transaction.

## Base template

```
Subject: Free DU previous-year paper resource for students

Hello,

I have created DU PYQ Online, a free resource that helps Delhi University
students find previous-year question papers and study material by course,
semester and subject:

https://www.dupyq.online/

I thought it could be useful for students visiting your website or resource
page. Please feel free to include it only if you believe it would genuinely
help your students.

Thank you,
Vinay
DU PYQ Online
```
